### Accepted corrections

- **Cancellation cost (Muse/Grok):** retired attempts reserve capacity until completion
  returns actual attempted pages. Only actual cost is charged, once. A late old result
  cannot finish a new attempt or replace its outcome.
- **Hourly allowance vs cycle cap (Opus A):** automatic work waits for a full 100-page
  allowance. A short hourly remainder cannot produce a terminal cycle partial. The
  300-page/hour ceiling and server Retry-After survive a scope transition.
- **Rejected partial reuse (Grok 1):** discard rejected aggregates before retry and on
  head/overlap/source instability. A subsequent budget failure cannot publish the
  previous rejected subtotal. A complete dated snapshot survives a later partial scan.
- **Futile retry (Opus C):** a changed primary summary returns unstable immediately;
  only page/head/period instability can repeat within the original shared budget.
- **Exact/canonical fingerprints (Muse):** decimal timestamp identity avoids Double
  rounding. Equivalent parsed cycle dates and normalized membership spellings compare
  equal. Distinct exact values remain distinct; malformed timestamps are not parsed by
  accepting a numeric prefix.
- **Old estimates beside new percentages (Muse/Grok):** retain dated amounts but withhold
  an old inferred limit when its pool's source percentage differs. Missing or spillover
  values block both caps. Same-value newer revisions do not needlessly discard a cap.
- **Metadata and missing-field enrichment (Grok 3/Opus):** publish coherent period-only
  percentages before history, retain them only under identical source ownership and
  fingerprint with a ten-minute bound, and retain their original source time. A separate
  missing-field fill is rate limited and backoff-aware. Primary and period precision
  histories are separate. A period failure may degrade exact family aggregation without
  fabricating a cap; empty/blank model lists are not evidence for inferred limits.
- **Unknown membership discovery (Opus B/H):** filling previously absent membership
  metadata does not change the active plan ownership key or strand an in-flight task.
  Known plan transitions still reset scope. Every cancelled path retires its reservation.
- **Explicit cache deletion (Opus D):** logout supplies a verified subject digest; the
  cache store compares it with the persisted envelope even before activation or after
  suspension. A different or unverified subject cannot delete that file.
- **Pending Bold (Grok 2):** an equal newer revision retains a still-valid pending Bold
  event with the original timestamp and expiry. New qualifying Bold replaces it; policy,
  continuity and ownership invalidation still cancel the applicable component.
- **Threshold correction while awaiting authorization (additional integration audit):**
  validate against the latest accepted candidates and current paid-cap identity before
  submission. A stale crossed threshold cannot survive a downward correction.
- **Fractional pp boundary (Opus F/Grok):** normalize a delta to the source's maximum
  three decimal places before comparing 5/15 pp. Values 4.999/14.999 stay below the next
  tier; 7.7→12.7 and 1.002→16.002 reach their exact intended boundaries.
- **Successful submission record (Opus G):** once submitted successfully, record exactly
  those threshold identities while ownership and lease remain valid. A later correction
  or unrelated policy edit cannot undo a delivered banner and make it repeat. Before
  submission the latest candidate/policy checks remain mandatory; logout prevents writes.

### Explicit scope and risk decisions

- **Conditional per-pool dollar jump attribution is deferred.** The primary pipeline
  has an aggregate included-dollar total; background historical scans do not bound
  every primary polling interval. This release consistently labels Included Usage Jump,
  retains the dollar delta and both available primary percentages, and states that
  per-pool dollar attribution is unavailable. No guessed allocation or delayed replay
  is introduced. This narrows the conditional Gate A path while preserving the user's
  dollar-sensitive Bold requirement.
- **Period fallback is display-only.** Threshold/jump signals use primary summary
  measurements. The source time and provenance are explicit; a late fill never becomes
  a second consumption event. This limitation is in the design and owner checklist.
- **Hard event/byte/time bounds remain terminal for automatic cycle scans**, with an
  explicit manual retry after cooldown. A slow connection may therefore need manual
  retry. The temporary hourly allowance is now distinct. This is a bounded-load tradeoff.
- **Unknown cost kinds and cross-page identical rows remain conservative uncertainty.**
  They can withhold estimates even at zero cost. Silently dropping or merging ambiguous
  events would claim evidence the undocumented source does not supply.
- Native installed-app operation remains the owner's next-day check. No model's review
  or synthetic screenshot is presented as a completed native delivery/hover test.
