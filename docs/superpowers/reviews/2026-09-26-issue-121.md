### Grok disposition

- **Blocker 1 (source evidence): resolved by the source-contract appendix**, which
  records already performed observation and synthetic schema. New source fields being
  absent from old code is expected at this gate.
- **Blocker 2 (optional endpoint auth): accepted explicit boundary.** Only the existing
  three primary endpoints participate in credential expiry. Period/monthly failures
  never delete credentials or log out a good primary session.
- **Blockers 3/4 (legacy effects/classification): added explicit split-only bypass and
  normative classification appendix.** Retain normalized ASCII lowercase + trim exact
  matching as a documented app policy, not an upstream guarantee. No substring or
  unbounded version matching. Cost-bearing contradictions are surfaced, not ignored.
- **Blocker 5 (JSONDecoder always rounds Decimal through Double): rejected after direct
  verification.** Installed Swift 6.4 decodes 0.1, 95.69, 46.984765,
  1234567890.1234567890123456789 and 1e-9 exactly as Decimal. Current Foundation's
  [Decimal decoding implementation](https://github.com/swiftlang/swift-foundation/blob/main/Sources/FoundationEssentials/JSON/JSONDecoder.swift)
  parses the numeric byte region directly. Keep a long-fraction regression test in the
  supported SDK CI matrix; do not add a custom JSON lexer based on an obsolete assumption.
- **Blocker 6 (identity fallback): narrowed persistence to verified nonempty subject.**
  Hashed normalized email may isolate in-memory state only. No subject means no new
  persistent amount cache or alert ledger; raw identity never enters those stores.
- **Blocker 7 (pruning): clarified cycle-end age**, not notification age. Current-cycle
  records survive day 8 and relaunch; bounded store validation is explicit.
- **Blockers 8/9 (recovery/legacy scope): clarified exact meaning.** Network/sleep or
  failed-delivery recovery does not rearm an already-delivered threshold. All new
  continuity/high-water rules apply only to split signals; legacy math stays intact.
- **Other accepted clarifications:** Summary Refresh amounts control, Korean split
  notification bodies, Summary scrolling, current-value vs threshold wording, explicit
  post-await policy checks and all new observation dependencies.
- **Mandatory successful teams lookup rejected:** documented personal accounts may
  reject that endpoint. Instead require the personal summary/request shape and reject
  known team/enterprise contradictions; do not make an unrelated optional endpoint a
  new authentication or quota authority.
