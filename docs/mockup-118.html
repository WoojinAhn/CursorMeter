A deliberately small view of your account. The latest 30 requests only. Full history and billing remain in Cursor’s dashboard. Other-device usage appears after the next successful account refresh, subject to Cursor reporting it.

Local follows this Mac’s time zone. Switching to UTC reformats the same saved data: no refresh, no new requests, and no change to the cache timestamp.

Design mockup with illustrative data. Refresh is simulated locally and does not contact Cursor. Controls outside the windows are for design review; native app code is unchanged.
